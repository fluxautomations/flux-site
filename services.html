Under the hood

What we build

Finance & Accounting

Receivables and payables, from the entry through to the money moving and the follow-up going out. Aging reports, bank file generation and payment advices stop being someone’s afternoon.

Invoice & document capture

Bills, receipts, delivery notes, scanned paperwork. Whatever arrives as a PDF or an image gets read once and logged straight into your database or ERP, in a format the rest of your systems can use.

ERP & API Integrations

The systems you already run get connected to each other. Data is pulled and pushed directly into ERPs like Tally via XML, so nothing gets entered twice.

Browser & Web Automations

Plenty of the portals a business depends on have no API at all. We drive the browser instead: pulling data, working through billing screens, filling the same form for the hundredth time, on a schedule rather than by hand.

Dashboards

Custom dashboards that refresh on a schedule and show the trends sitting inside your ERP data. Built around the handful of numbers you actually check, not around how many charts fit on a screen.

Nothing here is a product you buy off a shelf. Each build is scoped from what the audit finds in your own department, which is why two engagements rarely contain the same set of automations.
