About Flux Automations

RW

“People should do judgment work; machines should do the typing.”

Rahul Wagh · Founder, Flux Automations

Flux Automations was built on that premise. We specialise in finance back-office work: billing, payout and reconciliation pipelines that run without anyone retyping anything, plus the reporting and portal work that tends to sit around them.

We’ve sat inside accounts departments, so we know what the 7th of the month feels like. The reconciliation that won’t tie out. The vendor calling about a payment advice nobody sent. The owner asking for a number that’s still three days away.

That’s the work we take off your team. Not the judgment. The typing.

Every system is custom-built and production-grade, and it’s meant to run quietly in the background. Ideally the only thing you notice is the time you get back.
