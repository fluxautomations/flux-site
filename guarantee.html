Risk reversal

Our fee is always less than what we save you

“If the documented time savings in the first 90 days, valued at your team’s actual salary cost, don’t exceed our fee, we refund the difference.”

1. The baseline is signed before we build

The audit produces a Manual Hours Report: every repetitive task in your accounts department, the hours it takes each month, and what those hours cost at your team’s loaded salary. You read it. You correct anything that looks wrong. Then you sign it.

That signed document is the baseline. Everything the guarantee measures is measured against it — not against a number we invent afterwards.

2. Savings are measured the same way they were counted

Hours × loaded cost. The same tasks, the same salary figures, the same arithmetic as the baseline. We review it with you twice:

  • Day 45 — early read. If adoption is lagging or something is behaving differently than expected, we find out with time left to fix it.
  • Day 90 — the guarantee sign-off. We put the measured savings next to the fee, in writing.

3. The adoption clause

The guarantee holds while your team runs the system as agreed — the operator uses it at the frequency we agreed at the start. This is written into the contract and discussed openly in the sales meeting, not sprung on anyone afterwards.

We build the counter to this into the project itself: your operator approves the field mapping, gets two hours of training, and receives a printed Flux Certified Operator certificate. Nobody loses a job. The typing goes away; the control stays with your team.

4. What a refund actually is

If measured savings at day 90 come in below the fee, we refund the difference. Not the whole fee — the gap. The refund is capped at the fee.

We expect never to pay this out, because the audit numbers are deliberately conservative. The guarantee’s job is to move the risk off your side of the table before you sign — not to be exercised.

Why we can offer this and a freelancer can’t

Underwriting a guarantee like this requires an instrument that measures savings credibly — the audit, the signed baseline, the joint reviews. A script written for a fixed fee has no such instrument, which is why the promise stops at “it’ll save you time.”

It also requires that the system survives us leaving. Everything runs on your machines, with full source code, documentation and training handed over. If I get hit by a train, your system doesn’t know. That’s the whole point of local-first.

What’s covered

  • Every tier — Essential, Back-Office Suite and Autopilot — carries the same written savings guarantee.
  • The First Automation Sprint is Phase 1 of the Suite; the full fee is credited against the Suite if you proceed within 60 days.
  • Full documentation, staff training, source code and the CA-handoff pack are part of every engagement.
