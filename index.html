The Flux 3-Step

Audit → Automate → Handover

01

Audit

We sit in your accounts department for 60 to 90 minutes and measure exactly what manual work costs you in rupees.

You get a two or three page Manual Hours Report: hours per task, priced at your own salary cost, the automations that recover most of it, and the payback period. Yours to keep either way.

02

Automate

We build it and put it live on your own hardware, in days. Nothing reaches production until your operator has approved the field mapping.

03

Handover

You get the documentation, the training for whoever operates it, and support after go-live. From then on your team runs the system day to day. You own it outright, source code included.

Honest timeline: go-live takes 2 to 3 weeks including your team’s training. Not the “3 days” some vendors promise.
