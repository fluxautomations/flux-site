What moves the number

The difference between ₹65,000 and ₹2,75,000 is scope, not rate. How many workflows come in at once, whether a live dashboard is part of it, and how many departments beyond accounts are included.

If the budget is tight, we remove modules. We don’t reduce the rate. Said plainly in the meeting: “I can take modules out, but I can’t make the same work cheaper — the guarantee doesn’t survive corner-cutting.”

₹1,10,000 in perspective

The Back-Office Suite at ₹1,10,000 is roughly ₹300 a day over a year. Less than one data-entry salary for one month, and it never resigns.

That comparison only means something once you know your own number, which is what the audit produces before any fee is mentioned.

We don’t quote before the audit

No audit, no proposal. That isn’t a sales tactic, it’s a measurement problem. The guarantee is underwritten against your hours at your salary cost, and we can’t underwrite a number we haven’t measured.
